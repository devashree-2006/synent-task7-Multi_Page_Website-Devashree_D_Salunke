ABOUT REWEAR

Fashion doesn't
have to be disposable.

ReWear is built around a simple idea: the most sustainable clothing is often the clothing you already own.

WHY WE EXIST

We want to change
how we think about clothes.

Fashion moves quickly. Trends change, wardrobes grow, and clothes that still have plenty of life left in them often get forgotten.

ReWear encourages a different approach — wear what you have, share what you don't need, repair what you can, and transform what seems old.

Because extending the life of a piece of clothing is one small but meaningful step toward a more thoughtful fashion culture.
